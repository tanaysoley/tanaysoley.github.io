सुर

Sur

Sur listens to you sing and shows how close you are to each swar. It needs your microphone. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Use headphones if you turn on the tanpura, so the mic hears only you.

Settings

Sa (your tonic)
Swars to use

A thaat is a parent scale: just seven swars, the same going up and down. A raag is a melody built on a thaat, with its own way up (aroha) and down (avaroha); it can drop swars or use different ones in each direction.

Accuracy

Exactly on the swar is deep green. Halfway to the next swar is deep red. In practice mode, you count as on the swar while within this distance.
